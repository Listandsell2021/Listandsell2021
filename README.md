## 👋 Hallo, we're List & Sell

We're a **full-service digital agency headquartered in Berlin**, with teams in München, Potsdam, Düsseldorf, Hamburg and Frankfurt.
Business analysts, e-commerce managers, content managers, designers and developers work under one roof. We've delivered **several hundred projects**, and we don't stop at a nice-looking website: we build products that **bring in customers and sales**.

> *„Wir bauen nicht nur Webseiten, wir machen sie auch **erfolgreich**.“*
